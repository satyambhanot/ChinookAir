\section{Use-Case Scenarios}

Each scenario marks candidate classes with a \cls{single underline} and
candidate operations with a \op{double underline}. The two lists are
collected in the candidate tables of this section.

\subsection*{UC-06 Book Flight}

\begin{description}
\item[Actor] \cls{Customer}.
\item[Preconditions] The \cls{Customer} has an account and at least one
  \cls{Flight} with a free \cls{Seat} exists.
\item[Trigger] The \cls{Customer} wants to reserve a \cls{Seat} on a
  \cls{Flight}.
\end{description}

\paragraph{Main success scenario}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The \cls{Customer} \op{starts} the application.
\item The system \op{shows} the monthly \cls{Promotion} if this is the first
  time the application is opened this month.
\item The \cls{Customer} \op{logs in} with a username and a password.
\item The \cls{Customer} \op{enters} the origin \cls{Airport}, the destination
  \cls{Airport} and the date, and may choose an \cls{Airline}.
\item The system \op{lists} the \cls{Flight}s on that \cls{Route} with their
  departure times, prices and number of free \cls{Seat}s.
\item The \cls{Customer} \op{selects} a \cls{Flight}.
\item The system \op{shows} the seat map of the \cls{Aircraft} with the free
  \cls{Seat}s marked.
\item The \cls{Customer} \op{selects} a \cls{Seat}.
\item The system \op{creates} a \cls{Reservation} for the \cls{Customer}, with
  a confirmation number and the price of the \cls{Seat}, and marks it pending
  payment.
\item The \cls{Customer} \op{enters} the \cls{Payment} details: card type,
  cardholder name, card number and expiry date.
\item The system \op{simulates} the \cls{Payment} and \op{approves} it.
\item The system \op{confirms} the \cls{Reservation}.
\item The system \op{generates} the \cls{Confirmation}, \op{shows} it on screen
  and \op{saves} it as a text file.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Extensions}

\begin{description}
\item[3a. The username or password is wrong.] The system \op{shows} an error and
  asks again. The scenario continues at step 3.
\item[5a. No \cls{Flight} matches.] The system \op{tells} the \cls{Customer}
  that no \cls{Flight} was found. The scenario continues at step 4.
\item[9a. The \cls{Seat} was taken in the meantime.] The system \op{rejects}
  the \cls{Reservation}, \op{shows} a message and \op{shows} the seat map
  again. The scenario continues at step 8.
\item[10a. The card details are invalid.] The system \op{shows} a message that
  names the field and what is wrong. The scenario continues at step 10.
\item[11a. The \cls{Payment} is declined.] The system \op{shows} the reason and
  keeps the \cls{Reservation} pending payment. The \cls{Customer} either
  \op{retries}, and the scenario continues at step 10, or \op{abandons}. If the
  \cls{Customer} abandons, the system \op{cancels} the \cls{Reservation} and
  \op{releases} the \cls{Seat}, and the scenario ends.
\end{description}

\paragraph{Postconditions}

On success the \cls{Reservation} is confirmed, the \cls{Seat} is no longer free
for anyone else, the \cls{Payment} is recorded as approved with only the last
four card digits, and the \cls{Confirmation} file is saved. On every other
ending no \cls{Reservation} holds a \cls{Seat}.

\subsection*{UC-14 Manage Flights}

\begin{description}
\item[Actor] \cls{Administrator}.
\item[Preconditions] The \cls{Administrator} has an account, and at least one
  \cls{Airline}, \cls{Route} and \cls{Aircraft} exist.
\item[Trigger] The \cls{Administrator} wants to change the \cls{Flight}s that
  customers can reserve.
\end{description}

\paragraph{Main success scenario}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The \cls{Administrator} \op{logs in} with a username and a password.
\item The \cls{Administrator} \op{chooses} Manage Flights.
\item The \cls{Administrator} \op{chooses} Add Flight.
\item The \cls{Administrator} \op{picks} a \cls{Route}, an \cls{Airline} and an
  \cls{Aircraft}.
\item The \cls{Administrator} \op{enters} the flight number, the departure time,
  the arrival time and the base price.
\item The system \op{validates} the details: the flight number is new, the
  arrival is after the departure, the departure is in the future, and the
  \cls{Aircraft} is not on another \cls{Flight} at that time.
\item The system \op{creates} the \cls{Flight} and \op{creates} its \cls{Seat}s
  from the seat layout of the \cls{Aircraft}.
\item The \cls{Administrator} \op{updates} the schedule of an existing
  \cls{Flight} by entering a new departure time and a new arrival time.
\item The system \op{saves} the new schedule.
\item The \cls{Administrator} \op{removes} an outdated \cls{Flight}.
\item The system \op{deletes} the \cls{Flight} if it has no \cls{Reservation},
  and otherwise \op{archives} it so that old \cls{Reservation}s stay in the
  history.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Extensions}

\begin{description}
\item[4a. The \cls{Route} does not exist.] The \cls{Administrator} \op{adds} the
  \cls{Route} first. The scenario continues at step 4.
\item[6a. The flight number already exists or the times are not valid.] The
  system \op{shows} a message that names the problem. The scenario continues at
  step 5.
\item[6b. The \cls{Aircraft} is already on another \cls{Flight} at that time.]
  The system \op{shows} a message. The \cls{Administrator} picks another
  \cls{Aircraft} or other times, and the scenario continues at step 4.
\item[9a. The new times clash.] Another \cls{Flight} already uses the same
  \cls{Aircraft} at that time. The system \op{rejects} the change and
  \op{shows} a message. The scenario continues at step 8.
\item[11a. The \cls{Flight} still has active \cls{Reservation}s.] The system
  \op{blocks} the removal and \op{shows} a message. The \cls{Flight} stays
  unchanged and the scenario ends.
\end{description}

\paragraph{Postconditions}

On success the new \cls{Flight} and its \cls{Seat}s exist and customers can find
the \cls{Flight}, the changed schedule is saved, and the removed \cls{Flight}
no longer appears in searches or schedules. On every other ending nothing has
changed.
